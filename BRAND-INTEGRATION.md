# GEI-ERA V0.1.1 — YallToo Brand Integration

## Canonical brand relationship

**YallToo** is the parent/brand identity.

**GEI — Genesis Engineered Interpretations** is the intellectual platform and product ecosystem.

## Logo

The canonical YallToo logo supplied for GEI-ERA is the transparent 720×214 brand mark. The production shell references the canonical YallToo asset URL until a repository-hosted binary asset is installed.

## Usage

- Preserve the logo proportions.
- Never crop, stretch, recolor, or distort the supplied mark.
- Keep sufficient clear space around the logo.
- On mobile, constrain by width and preserve aspect ratio.
- Use the logo as the primary brand mark; GEI typography sits alongside it as the platform identifier.

## Brand hierarchy

YALLTOO → GEI → Control Room → Explore / Academy / Research / Laboratory / Library / Vault / Marketplace
