# V0.5 — GEI Research Engine

**Release:** 0.5.0  
**Repository:** `jawa-dam/GEI-ERA`  
**Status:** Implemented

## Purpose

V0.5 turns the V0.3 content architecture into a dedicated research environment. The Research Engine is designed to make GEI research records inspectable, traceable, and revision-friendly.

## Included

- Research repository interface at `research/index.html`
- Canonical research dataset at `research/research-data.js`
- Research state/filter engine at `research/research-engine.js`
- Mobile-first research visual system at `research/research.css`
- Papers and working papers
- Evidence records
- Source records
- Provenance metadata
- Audit-trail records
- Six-day chronology model
- Master chronology & taxonomy record
- Search and type/status filters
- Record inspection modal
- Review-state tracking in local browser storage
- Local research notes in local browser storage
- Stable research IDs and relationships

## Research integrity model

The interface deliberately distinguishes:

1. **Source** — what material is being referenced.
2. **Evidence** — what observation or supporting material is recorded.
3. **Claim** — what the researcher proposes.
4. **Interpretation** — how the claim is connected to the GEI framework.
5. **Review** — how the record is evaluated or revised.
6. **Provenance** — where the record came from and how it was created.

This structure does not establish the truth of a claim. It creates an auditable place to document claims and the evidence offered for them.

## V0.5 boundaries

No external publication database, peer-review identity system, payment processor, or server-side research database is introduced in this release. The browser-local review/note state is a prototype foundation. Future releases can connect these records to a canonical GitHub research repository, publication workflows, contributor identity, and persistent review services.
