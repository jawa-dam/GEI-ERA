Integrity boundary

Module Action Adapters provide explicit action contracts and routing hints. They do not authenticate users, prove identity, authorize protected access, verify payments, alter research evidence or claims, issue credentials, or create authoritative academic records. Module-owned systems remain responsible for their own state and security.
