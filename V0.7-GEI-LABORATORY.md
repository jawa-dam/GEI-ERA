# V0.7 — GEI Laboratory

## Purpose
V0.7 introduces an interactive laboratory layer connecting GEI systems thinking, water concepts, language observation, and Genesis learning experiences.

## Delivered
- Dam Gate Simulator with bounded conceptual water/pressure visualization.
- Laboratory experiment registry with stable IDs.
- Mill Simulator module definition.
- Water Lab module definition.
- Word Detective module definition.
- Genesis Systems Challenge module definition.
- Free/premium experiment access metadata.
- XP and completion tracking in browser-local development state.
- Responsive mobile-first Laboratory interface.
- Accessibility basics and reduced-motion handling.
- SEO metadata and canonical URL.

## Research and safety boundary
The simulators are educational conceptual models. They are not engineering design tools, hydraulic safety calculations, or evidence by themselves for historical or theological claims. Production scientific claims should remain linked to the GEI Research Engine's source, evidence, provenance, and review structures.
